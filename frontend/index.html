JAILBREAKERS

on-chain marketplace for reproducible model-failure findings · hashlock disclosure · staked, re-measured claims · contract on Sepolia

Red-teamers sell encrypted eval suites that make a named model exhibit a failure (a data leak, a jailbreak, a hallucination). Buyers — AI labs and deployers — pay before seeing the prompts. Payment releases on the seller's on-chain key reveal (that reveal is coordinated disclosure). After the resolution date a resolver re-runs the suite against the model and reports the measured failure rate: overfitted findings get their stake slashed and buyers rebated; malformed findings are refunded from stake via an on-chain dispute the contract adjudicates itself. Reputation counts only sold findings, so it can't be farmed.
