JAILBREAKERS

An on-chain market for proof that AI models fail.
Red-teamers sell encrypted eval suites that make a named model misbehave — e.g. leak a secret. Buyers pay into escrow before seeing the prompts; the seller reveals the key, which both releases payment and publicly discloses the finding. A resolver then re-runs the suite against the model and slashes claims that don't reproduce, while the contract itself rejects malformed suites. Reputation is earned only on findings that actually sold.
